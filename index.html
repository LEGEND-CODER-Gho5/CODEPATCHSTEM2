CodePatch STEM promotes accessible STEM education through computer science, mathematics, engineering, technology, student collaboration, workshops, and educational initiatives.

The organization encourages students to explore technology, develop technical knowledge, collaborate with others, and apply their learning through practical activities.

Four dimensions of STEM

Computer Science

Programming, computational thinking, and software development.

Think in steps, write them down, and watch a machine follow them.

Mathematics

Mathematical reasoning, problem-solving, and mathematical exploration.

Find the pattern, test whether it holds, then look for where it breaks.

Engineering

Design thinking, technical problem-solving, and practical experimentation.

Sketch it, build it, test it, and fix what fails.

Technology

Exploring emerging technologies and digital innovation.

Look at what is new, ask what it is good for, and try it.

    Questions

    What is CodePatch STEM?

    An organization that promotes accessible STEM education through computer science, mathematics, engineering, technology, student collaboration, workshops, and educational initiatives.

    What is a chapter?

    A school-based group of CodePatch STEM members. South Brunswick High School is our featured chapter.

    Do you run workshops?

    Workshops are part of our mission. Confirmed dates will appear in the events section, and Discord and Instagram carry announcements.

    How do I take part?

    Use the interest form above, or join the Discord server and say hello. You can explore coding, join mathematics activities, or help organize events.

    Can I start a chapter at my school?

    The chapter directory is built to grow. Use the interest form or Discord, mention your school, and say you want to start a chapter.
